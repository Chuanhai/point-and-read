Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Point & Read (the “App”) helps users recognize and hear printed words using an iPhone camera and speech technology. This policy describes how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Camera and printed text

The App requests camera permission to identify printed words. Camera frames and recognized text are processed on your device for the reading experience. The App does not require you to upload photos or create an account to use its core reading features.

2. Information stored on your device

The App may store local preferences and reading-usage information, such as language settings, celebration settings, and the number of distinct free words used. These settings support the App’s functionality and free-use allowance.

3. Subscriptions and payments

Optional subscriptions are purchased through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Apple processes payment information and manages subscription transactions. The App uses Apple StoreKit to check subscription status and enable paid features. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

4. Data sharing and analytics

The current App is designed to perform its core text recognition and speech functions on-device. We do not operate a server that receives camera images or recognized text for those features. Apple may process information independently in connection with App Store purchases and distribution under Apple’s own policies.

5. Children and families

The App can be used by families to support reading. It does not require children to create accounts or provide personal information for its core functionality. A parent or guardian should manage purchases and device permissions.

6. Your choices

You can revoke camera access in iOS Settings, manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings, and remove locally stored App data by deleting the App (subject to any data retained by Apple for purchases).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. The updated version will appear on this page with a revised date.
