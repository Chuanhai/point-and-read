Support

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Welcome to Point & Read Support. We’re here to help you recognize and hear printed words with your iPhone.

Getting started

  1. Open Point & Read and allow camera access.
  2. Point the center crosshair at a clearly printed word.
  3. Hold your phone steady briefly; the App recognizes and speaks the word.
  4. Tap the displayed word to hear it again, or choose a different reading language.

Frequently asked questions

Why isn’t a word recognized?

Try brighter lighting, a clearer printed font, holding the phone steady, or adjusting the zoom. Make sure the intended word is under the center crosshair.

Why is there no sound?

Check your iPhone volume and audio output, and verify that a voice for the selected language is available on your device.

How do I subscribe?

Tap the crown icon in the App, select Monthly or Yearly, and confirm your purchase using Apple’s payment sheet. The App should return to reading after a successful verified purchase.

How do I restore a purchase?

Tap the crown icon and select Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel?

Open Apple’s subscription management page, select Point & Read, and choose Cancel Subscription if available. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
